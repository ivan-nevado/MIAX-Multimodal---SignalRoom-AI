# Accounting reference (loaded by the Document Agent for uploaded reports)

- **Revenue** — top line; prefer total net revenue. Segment revenue must be labelled as such.
- **Operating income (EBIT)** — revenue minus operating expenses; operating margin = operating income / revenue.
- **Net income** — bottom line attributable to the company; net margin = net income / revenue.
- **GAAP vs non-GAAP** — earnings releases often show "adjusted" figures. Always label non-GAAP values.
- **EPS** — basic vs diluted; prefer diluted and label it.
- **Cash** — cash and cash equivalents; "cash and marketable securities" is a different, larger figure.
- **Debt** — long-term debt (+ current portion). Leases may or may not be included; say which.
- **Guidance** — forward-looking ranges stated by management (e.g. "Q3 revenue expected at $X ± 2%").
- **Periods** — fiscal years may not match calendar years (e.g. NVIDIA's fiscal year ends in late January).
- Never mix figures from different periods in one ratio.
