# Risk framework

| Category | HIGH when… | MEDIUM when… |
|---|---|---|
| regulatory | new rules/bans/probes directly targeting the company, multiple sources | proposals or sector-wide rules |
| valuation | evidence explicitly raises valuation stress after a large run-up | elevated multiples discussed |
| operational | supply disruption, recalls, outages reported | execution concerns |
| competition | material share loss / disruptive competitor reported | new entrants discussed |
| macro | rate/inflation/FX shock clearly relevant to the asset | general macro uncertainty |
| liquidity | thin volume, wide moves on low volume, funding stress | modest liquidity concerns |
| concentration | dependence on few customers/products reported | some concentration |
| governance | management turmoil, accounting issues | board/management changes |
| market | volatility_20d > 60% or |z| ≥ 3 | volatility_20d > 35% or |z| ≥ 2 |
